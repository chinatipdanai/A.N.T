## **Introduction (what,why)**

Universities and organizations have information about people, courses, projects, research, publications, skills, and industry roles. These data can be used to describe a person's talent and expertise, but relationships between them can be difficult to understand when shown only as lists or tables. For example, finding who has cybersecurity skills may require looking at their courses, projects, certifications, research, and experience, as well as comparing those skills with related industry roles.

**A.N.T. (Academic Network & Talent Map Platform)** is proposed to make these relationships easier to explore. The platform uses an interactive **Network Graph with a Force-Directed layout** to connect people, skills, courses, projects, research, publications, and industry roles. This allows users to see who has particular skills, what evidence supports those skills, which people or projects are related, and how available skills connect to industry requirements. KMUTT will be used as the initial case study, while the platform is designed to be adaptable for other organizations in the future.
